Where this comes from

Nothing on this page is typed by hand. The chart above is rebuilt by scripts/build-progress.mjs from status that scripts/scan-book.mjs computes out of events.json (what happened in each session) and nodes.json (which topics exist). To see why a topic is where it is, open its page: the coverage log there has the session-by-session detail. The Architecture page shows how the files fit together.

The hand-maintained per-subtopic tables that used to sit here were retired on 2026-09-20, once every written page was scored and so appears in the chart. They are still in the git history.

Built topic by topic. Depth over completion.
